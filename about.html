I'm a data professional with a foundation in Business Information Technology, Data Science and Analytics — the kind of person who gets genuinely excited about a clean dataset. I spend my days finding the patterns hiding in numbers and translating them into decisions people can actually act on.

What makes my approach a little different is the design background running underneath it: years of graphic and web design mean I don't just find the insight, I know how to present it so it lands. That same instinct — for clarity, structure, and things that just work for the person using them — is what's now pulling me toward software development. I'm currently a graduate trainee, learning to build the tools I used to only analyze.

How I like to work

Organized, collaborative, and quietly thorough. Whether I'm cleaning a messy dataset, coordinating a project timeline, or debugging a stubborn function, I'd rather ask one more clarifying question than guess. I take my work seriously — myself, somewhat less so.
